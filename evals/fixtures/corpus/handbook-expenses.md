# Expense and Reimbursement Policy

## What is covered

The reimbursement policy covers travel, accommodation, conference fees, and
equipment purchases under 500 euros. Anything above that threshold needs
written approval from your budget owner before purchase.

## How to file

Submit receipts within 30 days through the Concur portal. Reimbursements are
paid with the next payroll run after approval. Per-diem for international
travel is 65 euros per day without receipts.

## Company cards

Team leads carry company credit cards with a 3,000 euro monthly limit.
Personal use of a company card is a policy violation and triggers an audit.
