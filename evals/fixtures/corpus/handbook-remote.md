# Remote Work and Office Policy

## Hybrid default

Meridian Labs operates hybrid-first: employees work from anywhere in their
employment country and gather in person for quarterly team weeks. The Berlin
and Lisbon offices are open every weekday for anyone who prefers a desk.

## Equipment stipend

Remote employees receive a one-time 1,200 euro home office stipend and 40
euros monthly for internet costs. Stipend purchases belong to the employee.

## Working hours

Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 CET. Outside that window,
schedules are yours to shape; recurring meetings must land inside core hours.
