# Product Brief: The Daily Brief Feature

## Concept

The Daily Brief greets a user each morning with what changed while they
were away: documents edited by teammates, mentions to answer, meetings that
moved, and one suggested priority pulled from their task list.

## Scope for version one

Version one is read-only and email-delivered at 7:30 local time. It links
back into the product; it never takes actions on its own. Push and in-app
variants come later behind the same generation pipeline.

## Open questions

Whether weekend briefs are wanted at all, and whether the priority
suggestion should explain its reasoning inline or on hover.
