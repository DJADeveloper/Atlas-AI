# Guide: Code Review Expectations

## Response time

First review response within one business day; small PRs under 200 lines
get same-day attention. If you cannot review in time, say so in the thread
and name someone who can.

## What to look for

Correctness first, then clarity, then consistency. Style nits belong to
the linter, not the reviewer. A reviewer who requests changes owns a
follow-up review within a day of the update.

## Disagreements

Two rounds of disagreement escalate to a fifteen-minute synchronous call —
threads longer than that cost more than the decision usually deserves.
