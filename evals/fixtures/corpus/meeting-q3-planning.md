# Q3 Planning Offsite — Decisions

## Headcount

Engineering grows by six: two backend for the Ledger billing service, two
for the search rework, one SRE, and one security engineer. Design stays
flat until the research backlog clears.

## Budget

The infrastructure budget for Q3 is capped at 210,000 euros, a 9% increase,
with the entire increase earmarked for the ClickHouse analytics migration
and GPU time for embedding experiments.

## Cut lines

The mobile offline mode and the Salesforce connector both slip to Q4. The
team agreed that shipping Orion search well beats shipping three things
badly.
