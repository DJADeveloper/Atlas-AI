# Guide: Engineering Interview Process

## Stages

The loop is four stages: a 30-minute recruiter screen, a take-home task
capped at three hours, a technical deep-dive on the candidate's own
submission, and a values conversation with two future teammates.

## The take-home rule

Take-homes are reviewed anonymously and paid with a 150 euro voucher.
Candidates who prefer live coding can choose a pairing session instead —
both paths are weighted identically.

## Decisions

The panel decides within two business days of the final stage. A split
panel defaults to no-hire; we would rather re-run a search than average
our way into a maybe.
