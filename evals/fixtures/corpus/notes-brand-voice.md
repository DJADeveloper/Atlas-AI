# Brand Voice Guidelines

## Principles

Meridian writes like a helpful senior colleague: plain sentences, concrete
examples, no exclamation-mark enthusiasm. We say what a feature cannot do
as clearly as what it can.

## Words we avoid

Avoid "revolutionary", "seamless", "AI-powered magic", and any claim of
"military-grade" anything. Numbers beat adjectives: "answers in under a
second" outsells "blazingly fast".

## Error messages

Errors apologize once, state what happened, and offer the next step.
Blame the system, never the user, and always keep the user's data visible.
