# Onboarding Guide for New Joiners

## First week

Your first week at Meridian Labs pairs you with an onboarding buddy, ships
your laptop, and walks through the architecture spine document. Payroll and
benefits enrollment happen on day two with the People team.

## First month

By the end of month one you should have shipped a starter task in your
team's codebase, met your five closest collaborators, and agreed on a
30-60-90 plan with your manager.

## Probation

Probation lasts six months with a written check-in at month three. The
check-in covers delivery, collaboration, and growth areas — no surprises.
