# Incident Response Runbook

## Severity levels

Severity 1 means customer-facing outage or data loss risk; severity 2 means
degraded service with a workaround; severity 3 is internal-only impact. Only
sev-1 pages the on-call engineer at night.

## The first fifteen minutes

The responder's first job is stabilization, not diagnosis: declare the
incident in the #incidents channel, appoint a communications lead, and start
the incident document from the template. Updates go out every 30 minutes.

## Postmortems

Blameless postmortems are due within five working days. Action items get
owners and dates, and the reliability guild reviews overdue items monthly.
