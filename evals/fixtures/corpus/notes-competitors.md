# Competitive Landscape Notes

## The three we watch

Fieldstone dominates enterprise deals with compliance depth we lack;
Quillworks wins small teams on price and onboarding speed; and Novagraph's
knowledge-graph angle wows demos but reviews suggest heavy setup costs.

## Our wedge

Meridian wins when evaluation criteria include retrieval quality and local
processing. We lose most often on missing SSO features and on procurement
timelines, not on the product's core.

## Watch items

Quillworks is hiring search engineers aggressively; Fieldstone acquired a
vector database startup in February. Reassess positioning at the next
quarterly review.
