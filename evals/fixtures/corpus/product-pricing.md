# Pricing Model Notes

## Current tiers

Meridian sells three tiers: Solo at 12 euros per seat, Team at 29 euros per
seat with a ten-seat minimum, and Enterprise as annual contracts starting
at 40,000 euros with SSO, audit logs, and a dedicated success manager.

## The usage question

Finance is modeling a usage-based component for API-heavy customers because
the top 4% of workspaces generate 31% of infrastructure cost. Any usage
meter must be predictable: hard caps with alerts, never surprise invoices.

## Grandfathering

Existing customers keep their current per-seat price for twelve months
after any pricing change, announced ninety days in advance.
