# On-Call Rotation and Compensation

## Rotation shape

Each product area runs a weekly on-call rotation with a primary and a
shadow. Handoff happens Mondays at 10:00 with a written summary of open
alerts and ongoing risks.

## Compensation

On-call weeks are compensated with 400 euros plus a recovery day after any
night with more than two pages. Swaps are self-service in the PagerDuty
schedule as long as coverage stays unbroken.

## Alert hygiene

Any alert that pages twice without requiring action must be tuned or deleted
within the same week. The alert review board meets every second Thursday.
