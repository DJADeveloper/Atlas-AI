# Time Off and Leave Policy

## Vacation allowance

Every full-time employee at Meridian Labs receives 25 days of paid vacation
per calendar year, accruing monthly from the first day of employment. Unused
days roll over up to a maximum of 10 days into the next year.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is unlimited but requires a doctor's note after five consecutive
days. Extended medical leave beyond three weeks moves to the long-term
disability process managed by the People team.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 20 weeks fully paid; secondary caregivers receive
8 weeks. Leave can be split into two blocks within the child's first year.
